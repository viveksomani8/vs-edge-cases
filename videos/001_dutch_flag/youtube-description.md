Rearrange an array into three groups (smaller than a pivot, equal to it, larger) in place and in a single pass. The idea is simple; the index bookkeeping is where most people slip. This video walks through the algorithm step by step with three pointers, shows why each one moves when it does, and explains where the algorithm is used in practice and why interviewers like it.

You will learn:
- the brute-force solution and why it costs O(n) extra space
- the four-region invariant that makes the one-pass solution easy to get right
- why the "equal" pointer stays put after a swap with "larger", but advances after a swap with "smaller"
- how this is the partition step of quicksort for data with many duplicates
